ABOUT LIBAS TAILOR

Rooted in Aligarh. Tailored for today.

LIBAS TAILOR is a menswear tailoring house in Aligarh, specialising in Sherwanis, bespoke suits and occasion wear.

01 • THE PHILOSOPHY

The Atelier

At LIBAS TAILOR, tailoring is treated as a personal dialogue between human anatomy and master craftsmanship. We do not believe in mass factory production or one-size-fits-all adjustments.

Every garment is conceived around the client’s natural stance, shoulder pitch, and spine curvature, ensuring effortless poise and comfort throughout long wedding celebrations and formal events.

02 • CROWN SPECIALIZATION

Known for Sherwanis in Aligarh

The Sherwani is at the very core of LIBAS TAILOR. Over years of focused practice at Shamshad Market, our atelier has earned a dedicated reputation in Aligarh for majestic wedding coats.

From pure Banarasi raw silks with subtle tone-on-tone embroidery to modern structured bandhgalas, every piece is balanced to look commanding under venue lighting and feel comfortable through every ceremony ritual.

03 • ATELIER DISCIPLINES

The Craft

Precision, restraint, and durability in every stitch.

01 • FIT & PROPORTION

Individual Paper Drafting

We cut an individual pattern from zero for every client. Shoulder slope, back pitch, and chest volume are drafted precisely to eliminate pulling.

02 • FABRIC CURATION

Celebratory Weaves

We guide fabric selection based on season, celebration timing, and venue climate—curating rich raw silks, velvets, breathable wools, and fine linens.

03 • INTERNAL ARCHITECTURE

Internal Canvassing

Tailored chest interlinings maintain structured form across long celebration hours while naturally breathing and following your body movement.

04 • HERITAGE & COMMUNITY

The Aligarh Connection

Situated at Shamshad Market, directly opposite Sulaiman Hall near Aligarh Muslim University (AMU), LIBAS TAILOR is an authentic local institution. We proudly serve generations of students, professors, professionals, and wedding families throughout Aligarh and western Uttar Pradesh.
