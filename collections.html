01 • CROWN SPECIALIZATION

The Royal Sherwani

“Known for Sherwanis in Aligarh.”

OCCASION: WEDDINGS & CEREMONIES

Hand-Crafted Grandeur

LIBAS TAILOR creates bespoke Sherwanis in Aligarh, spanning authentic ceremonial traditions to contemporary silhouettes. Cut from individual paper patterns drafted to your posture, our Sherwanis are hand-tailored in pure Banarasi raw silks, celebratory jacquards, and royal velvets with tailored internal interlining.

Construction: Tailored Internal Interlining & Canvassing
Primary Weaves: Banarasi Raw Silk, Jacquard & Velvet
Collar: Hand-Bound Mandarin / Nehru
TAILORING SILHOUETTE • HERITAGE LINE

The Aligarh Cut

Characterized by an authoritative chest structure, a clean high-set collar roll, and an unbroken vertical drape. Rather than stiff industrial padding, our Aligarh-cut Sherwanis rely on anatomical chest canvassing and subtle waist shaping that moves effortlessly with you during long wedding rituals.
