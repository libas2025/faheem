LEGAL & POLICIES

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 • LIBAS TAILOR, Aligarh

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with LIBAS TAILOR through our digital channels (including booking consultation requests, submitting bespoke measurement profiles, or messaging via WhatsApp), we may collect your full name, telephone / WhatsApp number, email address, physical address or fitting location, anatomical measurement parameters, and custom garment design preferences.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

Your information is collected solely for the purposes of:

  • Scheduling in-person consultation sessions at our Shamshad Market atelier or home visits in Aligarh.
  • Drafting custom paper patterns and crafting bespoke sherwanis, suits, and occasion garments.
  • Communicating order milestones, fitting appointments, and delivery coordination.
  • Archiving your measurement record for future bespoke reorders upon your request.

3. Storage & Confidentiality

Client measurement records and contact details are maintained confidentially within our atelier. We do not sell, rent, lease, or publicly disclose your personal contact information or anatomical measurements to any third-party marketing companies.

4. Third-Party Links & WhatsApp Communication

Our website provides direct communication channels via WhatsApp and location mapping via Google Maps. By utilizing these features, you engage with their respective independent platforms governed by their own privacy terms.

5. Client Rights & Inquiries

You may at any time request an update, review, or deletion of your saved measurement profile by contacting our master tailor directly:
